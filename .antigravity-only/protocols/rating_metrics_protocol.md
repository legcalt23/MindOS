## Metric review dimensions

Every rating/metric plan must review:

- Validity.
- Reliability.
- Measurement error.
- Score stability.
- Learning effects.
- Practice/farming effects.
- Fatigue effects.
- Device bias.
- Cross-platform comparability.
- Rating inflation.
- Rating decay.
- Cold start.
- Uncertainty intervals.
- Per-mode separation.
- Global aggregation risk.

## Recommended metric candidates

- Accuracy.
- Reaction time.
- Inverse efficiency score.
- Consistency.
- Volatility.
- Fatigue slope.
- Improvement rate.
- Retention.
- Difficulty reached.
- Error type.
- Lapses.
- Learning curve.
- Stability under pressure.

## Metric integrity rule

Premium must never affect rating, ranking, measured difficulty, scoring, or competitive fairness.
