# Review Cognitive Claims

## Allowed
- Mindboost trains functional cognitive skills.
- Mindboost tracks your progress over time.
- Mindboost adapts difficulty based on performance.
- Mindboost helps you practice attention, memory, reasoning and metacognition.

## Risky
- Mindboost makes you smarter.
- Mindboost improves intelligence.
- Mindboost enhances your brain.

These require qualification and measurement context.

## Forbidden
- Mindboost diagnoses ADHD.
- Mindboost treats cognitive disorders.
- Mindboost guarantees IQ improvement.
- Mindboost clinically improves intelligence.
- Mindboost replaces a professional evaluation.

## Output
1. Claim reviewed
2. Risk level
3. Problem
4. Safer rewrite
5. Why the rewrite is safer
