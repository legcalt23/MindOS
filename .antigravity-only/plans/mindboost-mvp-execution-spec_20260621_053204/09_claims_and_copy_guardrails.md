# Claims and Copy Guardrails

---

## 1. Allowed Public Language (Approved Copy)

Use the following phrases in marketing materials, websites, and store listings:
*   *"Attentional telemetry for high-stakes builders."*
*   *"Measure daily cognitive baseline variance before you start your session."*
*   *"Train cognitive control under adaptive heuristic changes."*
*   *"Offline-first, local-only attentional performance telemetry that respects your data privacy."*
*   *"No health claims. No IQ tests. Just raw reaction speed and consistency metrics."*

---

## 2. Prohibited Claims (Blacklisted Copy)

Never use these terms or concepts in public communication:
*   **Do NOT say:** *"Cures brain fog, treats ADHD, or prevents cognitive decline."* (Triggers FDA/medical regulatory reviews).
*   **Do NOT say:** *"Boosts your IQ or makes you smarter in 10 days."* (Unproven, FTC target).
*   **Do NOT say:** *"We track your stress and sleep to optimize your brain."* (Implies background surveillance).
*   **Do NOT say:** *"Compare your brain power to other developers."* (Breaks the within-user comparison policy).

---

## 3. In-App Wording Guidelines

*   **When SRI is Low:**
    *   *Risky:* *"Warning: Cognitive function compromised. You are unfit to work."*
    *   *Safe:* *"Attentional Drift Detected. Reaction speed consistency is lower than your 7-day average. Consider reviewing sleep, caffeine, or scheduling deep-focus work later."*
*   **When introducing AHS (Swapped targets):**
    *   *Risky:* *"Rule swap: Confuse your brain now!"*
    *   *Safe:* *"Heuristic mutation active. Buttons swapped to prevent automated motor habits."*

---

## 4. Onboarding Disclaimers

Every user must see this text before baseline calibration:
> **Core Disclaimer**  
> *"MindBoost is a cognitive readiness tool that measures daily reaction speed variance relative to your own baseline. It is not a clinical test, is not design to diagnose or treat ADHD, anxiety, or sleep disorders, and does not provide an IQ score. All metrics are stored locally on your device."*

---
