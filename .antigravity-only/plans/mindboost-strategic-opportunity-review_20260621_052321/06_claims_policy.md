# Claims Policy

This document regulates the marketing language, public claims, and disclaimers to prevent FTC, FDA, and legal regulatory liabilities.

---

## 1. Claims Classification

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             CLAIMS HIERARCHY                             |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  ALLOWED NOW:                                                            |
|  - Deterministic measurements (e.g., "Reaction speed is measured in ms")  |
|  - Relative baseline tracking ("Compare today's performance to yesterday") |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  EVIDENCE-REQUIRED:                                                      |
|  - "Reduces attentional drift by X%"                                      |
|  - "Improves task-switching speed in professional tasks"                 |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  PROHIBITED (NEVER USE):                                                 |
|  - "Reverses cognitive decline or brain aging"                           |
|  - "Treats ADHD, depression, or burnout"                                 |
|  - "Increases general IQ / Fluid intelligence scores"                    |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### A. Prohibited Claims (No-Go):
*   **Medical/Clinical Diagnoses:** Do not claim the app can diagnose, screen, or treat ADHD, mild cognitive impairment, dementia, anxiety, depression, or sleep disorders.
*   **General Intelligence / IQ Gains:** Do not claim the training increases general IQ or fluid intelligence. (The scientific consensus is highly skeptical of "general transfer" of brain games to IQ).
*   **Unverified transfer claims:** Do not claim that "playing MindBoost makes you a better coder" without running studies that measure real-world coding performance improvements.

### B. Allowed Claims (Safe):
*   **Deterministic Calibration:** *"MindBoost tracks millisecond-level variations in simple and choice reaction times."*
*   **State-Locked Telemetry:** *"Tracks your attention consistency using the Coefficient of Variation, which is standard in psychomotor vigilance research."*
*   **Cognitive Workout:** *"Bypasses muscle-memory automation using dynamic rule mutations (Heuristic Disruption) to keep the executive control system engaged."*
*   **Personal Baseline Comparison:** *"Measures daily fluctuations in your cognitive speed and accuracy relative to your own historical baseline."*

---

## 2. Safe Marketing Copy vs. Risky Copy

*   **RISKY:** *"Unleash your brain's hidden IQ. MindBoost cures brain fog and boosts focus for coders."*
*   **SAFE:** *"Attentional telemetry for builders. Measure your reaction latency and attention stability before starting your work session."*
*   **RISKY:** *"Prevent cognitive decline and ADHD distraction."*
*   **SAFE:** *"Train your ability to quickly switch between cognitive rules under high-difficulty adaptive staircases."*

## 3. Mandatory Disclaimers

### A. App Store & Onboarding Footer:
> *"MindBoost is a cognitive telemetry and training tool for self-optimization. It is not a medical device, is not cleared by the FDA/EMA, and is not designed to diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any neurological condition or psychiatric disorder."*

### B. Crisis Mode Disclaimer:
> *"Stress tolerance audits induce high cognitive load, time compression, and auditory/visual stress. If you have a history of panic attacks, cardiac sensitivity, or sensory overload issues, please consult your physician before using this training mode."*

### C. Data Privacy Disclaimer:
> *"All cognitive tests, trial data, raw timing logs, and calculated metrics are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device. MindBoost does not upload your raw cognitive telemetry to external servers."*
