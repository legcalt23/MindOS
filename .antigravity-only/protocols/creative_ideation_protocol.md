# Creative Ideation Protocol

## Mission

Generate a powerful idea field before converging into implementation plans.

## Mandatory output

For every major plan:

```text
20 conservative ideas
20 ambitious ideas
20 wild-but-possible ideas
```

## Classification tags

Every idea must receive at least three tags:

```text
Safe to plan now
Experimental
High-risk/high-upside
Scientifically uncertain
Monetizable
Retention-focused
Competitive differentiation
Requires validation
MVP-compatible
Post-MVP
B2B potential
Premium-safe
Metric-sensitive
Needs Codex review
```

## Required critique pass

After ideation, each idea must be reviewed by:

- Cognitive Science Lead.
- Exercise Optimization Lead if exercise-related.
- Psychometrics & Measurement Scientist if metric-related.
- Financial Strategy Partner.
- Product Strategy Lead.
- Privacy & Safety Guard.
- Claims & Terminology Gatekeeper.
