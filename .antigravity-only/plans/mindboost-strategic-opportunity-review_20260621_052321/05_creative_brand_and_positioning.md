# Creative, Brand, and Positioning

This document establishes the creative direction, brand identity, naming taxonomy, and user experience concepts for MindBoost.

---

## 1. Recommended Category & Brand Narrative

### Category:
**Cognitive Readiness Systems (CRS)** or **Executive Function Diagnostics**. 

### Brand Narrative:
In high-performance engineering, trading, and operations, we monitor every server, market indicator, and infrastructure node. Yet, the most critical node—the human brain—runs completely unmonitored. 

MindBoost is not a gym to "get smarter." It is a diagnostic dashboard that monitors the reliability of your brain's processing capacity. By measuring raw physical reaction speeds and attentional stability in real-time, MindBoost ensures that you perform only when you are at your peak, and warns you before attentional drift leads to critical errors.

## 4. Onboarding Concept

*   **Step 1 (Context):** The user is asked for their role (e.g., Engineer, Trader, Founder) and their primary cognitive risk (e.g., attentional errors, speed slowdown, fatigue).
*   **Step 2 (The Clean Baseline):** The user runs their first 3-minute calibration. The app explains: *"We are measuring your baseline reaction time and consistency. Relax and tap as fast as possible when the light flashes."*
*   **Step 3 (The Pattern Break):** During the second half of calibration, the target swap rule activates. The user experiences a sudden, hard rule change. The screen shows: *"Heuristic shift detected. Your brain had to shift processing back to executive control. This is the foundation of cognitive training."*
*   **Step 4 (Setup Local DB):** The onboarding completes by generating their local secure database. No email is requested for local runs, maximizing trust.
