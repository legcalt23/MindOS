# Final Sign-Off

This document formalizes the final strategic and scientific sign-off for the MindBoost product direction.

## 1. Core Strategic Directives

*   **Product Definition:** A Cognitive Readiness Diagnostic Dashboard.
*   **Target Segment:** Developers, Builders, and Operators.
*   **Monetization Mode:** Premium SaaS (Utility-First).

## 2. Core Recommendations Summary

### A. MVP Recommendation:
Build [E1 (Destello)](file:///c:/Users/curro/AndroidStudioProjects/MindOS/MindBoost_Velocidad_Mental_Plan_v2_2_3.md#L37) and [E6 (Multivía)](file:///c:/Users/curro/AndroidStudioProjects/MindOS/MindBoost_Velocidad_Mental_Plan_v2_2_3.md#L37) using sub-millisecond [ANDROID_UI_UPTIME_NANOS](file:///c:/Users/curro/AndroidStudioProjects/MindOS/MindBoost_Velocidad_Mental_Plan_v2_2_3.md#L3) clocks. Implement **Heuristic Disruption Engine (AHS)** as the core adaptive mechanism. Calculate relative daily variance (System Readiness Index) on-device.

### B. Claims Recommendation:
Strict prohibition of clinical, cognitive-recovery, or general IQ claims. Marketing copy must center on **cognitive readiness telemetry, baseline variance tracking, and attention control under stress**.

### C. Scientific Recommendation:
Validate the System Readiness Index by correlating it with Psychomotor Vigilance Task (PVT) baselines in a cohort of sleep-deprived testers before publicly advertising the score's diagnostic utility.

### D. Commercial Recommendation:
Focus on the developer/trader segment as the initial beachhead. Avoid mass-market wellness pricing. Start with a premium annual model ($99/year) to signal professional utility.

### E. Security & Privacy Recommendation:
Store all raw trial telemetry locally in Room DB using SQLCipher encryption. Reject background keystroke or camera-based eye-tracking systems to protect developer privacy.

### **CONDITIONAL GO-DECISION**

*   **Sign-Off Trigger:** The project can progress to Phase 1 (MVP implementation) only after the engineering team validates that the monotonic clock timebases align perfectly across different Android testing configurations.
*   **Prohibited Action:** Any implementation of background telemetry tracking (SCLE) or IDE extensions (EWO) is strictly barred from current sprints.
