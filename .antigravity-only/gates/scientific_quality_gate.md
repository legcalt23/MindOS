# Scientific Quality Gate

**Role:** Ensures that all plans, models, and features meet strict scientific quality standards before handoff.

**Rules:**
- Validates the Evidence Level (High, Medium, Low, Experimental, Creative, Rejected).
- Ensures no pseudo-science, clinical claims, or misleading metrics are included.
- Enforces that **Science always beats creativity**.
- Validates the required metrics, limits of evidence, and risk of overclaiming.
