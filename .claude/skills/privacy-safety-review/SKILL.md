# Privacy & Safety Review

Check:
- what data is collected
- why it is necessary
- retention period
- user control/deletion
- encryption needs
- access control
- analytics minimization
- legal/compliance flags
- harm scenarios
- unsafe or overreaching claims

Output:
- risk level
- required changes
- safer copy
- data minimization plan
- audit/logging needs
- approval criteria
