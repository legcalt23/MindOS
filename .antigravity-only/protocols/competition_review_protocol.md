# Competition Review Protocol

## Competition progression ladder

```text
1. Training
2. Offline Simulation
3. Shadow Candidate
4. Live Shadow Ranked Beta
5. Private Ranked Beta
6. Public Ranked
7. Tournaments
```

No exercise should enter Public Ranked or Tournaments without passing scientific, fairness, metric integrity, privacy, safety, and Codex security review.

- Skill divisions.

## Hard block conditions

Block public competition if:

- The task is highly device-dependent.
- The rating model is not stable.
- Premium can alter measured opportunity unfairly.
- Cheating risk is material and unreviewed by Codex.
- Scientific construct is unclear.
- The user-facing claim exceeds evidence.
